Characteristics

Multishot increases weapon damage instead of creating additional projectiles.
Damage bonus is multiplicative to other sources of damage.
Arbucep's main and component blueprints are acquired from Deepmines Bounties as an uncommon and rare drop. Access requires completion of The New War.
Alternatively Arbucep's blueprints can be purchased from Nightcap in Fortuna with Fergolyte from Deepmines Bounties. Each component blueprint requires 150 and the main blueprint requires 220, totaling 670 Fergolytes for the whole set. Players must be at Rank 3 - Seeker with Nightcap in order to purchase.

## Cortege
An ancient weapon designed by the Entrati for use by their Necramechs. Primary fire siphons life essence from the target to fuel a devastating secondary fire. A heavy flamethrower with surprising range. Secondary fire launches three projectiles in a fan pattern that explode, leaving a damaging area of effect for a short duration.

### Wiki
Orokin
An ancient weapon designed by the Entrati for use by their Necramechs. Primary fire siphons life essence from the target to fuel a devastating alt fire. A heavy flamethrower with surprising range. Alt fire launches three projectiles in a fan pattern that explode, leaving a damaging area of effect for a short duration.
The Cortege is an Orokin Era weapon created by the Entrati, a beam Archgun that builds charges on kills to allow use of the Alternate Fire, a trio of grenades that leaves lingering flames.
Characteristics
This weapon deals primarily Heat damage on its primary fire and Blast damage on its Alternate Fire.
Primary fire releases a flamethrower-like beam.
Pinpoint accuracy.
Consumes 0.5 ammo per tick of damage.
Beam has a thickness of ~1 meter.
Beam deals radial damage varying from of 1 to 4 meters in a cone shape.
The Atmospheric beam has innate Infinite Body Punch Through.
No Damage Falloff in Archwing Mode.
Getting 5 kills with the Cortege's primary fire will unlock an Alternate Fire that releases a trio of grenades that explodes in an 4 meter radius on impacting a surface or when remotely detonated with Alternate Fire, leaving lingering flames that deal continuous damage for 11 seconds.
Initial hit and explosion apply status separately.
Explosion does not need direct line of sight to deal damage and will penetrate walls.
Consumes no ammo.
After using Alternate Fire, additional kills are needed to recharge the grenade barrage.

## Corvas
When fully charged, this flak cannon delivers a devastating shot. Perfect for taking down fast-moving interceptors.

### Wiki

Atmosphere:

Atmosphere:

Corvas Prime
**Acquisition:**
The Corvas's blueprint can be purchased from the Market. Its parts can be purchased from various Syndicates. All parts, except for the blueprint, are tradeable.
**Notes:**
In Archwing mode, Corvas fires a wide energy projectile, similar to the Arca Plasmor. In Atmospheric mode, it functions like a shotgun, and fires hit-scan pellets.
**Tips:**
As a shotgun-like weapon, the Corvas is best used when engaging enemies at close range, where its scattershot spread can be used to deal massive damage against enemy units.
Charging this weapon is not advised, as after modding for fire rate, the added charge time reduces total dps more than the higher base damage compensates for it. In atmosphere, the fully-charged stagger further feeds the disparity.

## Corvas Prime
Flaunting ceremonial beauty, this arch gun flak cannon is even more devastating than its standard issue counterpart.

### Wiki
Weapons
Flaunting ceremonial beauty, this arch-gun flak-cannon is even more devastating than its standard issue counterpart.
Corvas Prime is the Primed variant of the Corvas Archgun flak-cannon, sporting improvements to its Damage, Critical Chance, Status Chance, Reload Speed, and Atmospheric Damage Falloff, with some expense to Magazine Size. Instead of firing shotgun-like rounds, the Corvas Prime shoots single, large Arca Plasmor-like projectiles whether it's used in space or solid ground. It was released alongside Garuda Prime and Nagantaka Prime.
Characteristics
This weapon deals primarily Heat damage.
Shots have a guaranteed Impact proc.
Two innate Madurai and a single Naramon polarity.
Corvas
**Notes:**
The projectile fired is similar to the Arca Plasmor, a large projectile that can hit lines of enemies if the center of the projectile doesn't directly impact enemies, allowing the surrounding wave to hit anyone within its small radius as it travels.
Equipping Sabot Rounds can compensate for instances of a direct hit.
**Tips:**
With its high damage and ability to hit the head of enemies if aimed above enemy heads, Archgun Ace can be easy to proc giving you bonus fire/charge rate as well as reducing the delay before the ammo begins to regenerate. While the buff cannot refresh, as long as the buff is active when you stop firing the delay reduction will still be applied.
Charged shots will stagger the player in Heavy Weapon mode, so normal shots are recommended instead while saving the higher powered charged shots for Necramechs as they are unaffected by staggering effects.
When used against Profit-Taker, loading Corvas Prime with Sabot Rounds allows it to take out two or even three legs at the same time with well angled close range shots.
**Trivia:**
Corvas Prime is the first Primed Archgun.

### Wiki

Delivering twin streams of highly accurate, rapid-fire ordnance, the Decurion are specifically designed for combat in the vacuum of space.

Characteristics

Good ammo efficiency, generating relatively small cooldown for the Archgun Deployer for every second fired.

Prisma Dual Decurions
**Acquisition:**
The Dual Decurion's blueprint can be purchased from the Market. Its parts can be purchased from various Syndicates. All parts, except for the blueprint, are tradeable.
**Notes:**
Despite the appearance of its shots, the Dual Decurion is a hitscan weapon by default.
Starting from Update 27, all hitscan Archwing weapons are converted to projectile when in space. Such weapons (Dual Decurion included) still retain their hitscan properties while deployed in ground missions with an Archgun Deployer.
The Dual Decurion's smaller magazine makes it easy to run out of ammo in a gunfight, but its quick ammo recharge balances this out.
A good way to maintain ammunition supply is to fire in bursts of 2-8 bullets.
Its component parts are listed in Offerings as "Decurion" parts, and Dual is not in the parts' name.
**Trivia:**
A Decurion was a Roman cavalry officer originally in command of 10 men.
It is one of several Dual/Akimbo weapons in Warframe with no solo counterpart, including the Twin Gremlins, Akjagara, Akzani, Twin Basolk, Dual Cleavers, Dual Ichor, Dual Zoren.
This also can be applied to Dakra Prime and Dex Dakra which have no default version even though an original version is implied by the name.
When used by Necramechs, instead of dual wielding it only uses a single Decurion.

### Wiki

Characteristics
This weapon deals primarily Slash damage.
Innate infinite punch through.
Inflicts a guaranteed Impact proc.
Projectiles have a very wide hitbox.
Projectiles have travel time.
Limited range of 450 meters in Archwing mode.
Limited range of 55 meters in Atmospheric mode.

**Acquisition:**
The Fluctus's blueprint can be researched from the Tenno Lab in the dojo. Its parts can be purchased from various Syndicates. All parts, except for the blueprint, are tradeable.
**Notes:**
The Fluctus' large projectile hitbox makes it a good mid-ranged crowd-control weapon especially when doing Archwing Interception and Defense missions as enemies come in in waves.
The Fluctus' projectiles also bypass the barriers protecting Shield Dargyns (and their Hellion counterparts) from front attacks, giving the weapon an edge over most Archguns when dealing with such units.
This also allows players to safely defeat them from range instead of trying to go into melee, as getting into contact with their shields will cause Electricity damage that can become particularly fatal at higher levels.
**Tips:**
Due to its infinite punch through, the Fluctus is a viable option as a Necramech weapon for Orphix runs as it can easily hit the Resonators that are hiding behind barriers.
