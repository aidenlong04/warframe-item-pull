## Arcane Acceleration

### Wiki
Arcane Acceleration is an Arcane Enhancement that provides a 30% chance on each Critical Hit to increase the fire rate of non-shotgun primary weapons for 9 seconds.
Can be sold for 500.
Effect
Awarded for killing or capturing the Eidolon Gantulyst.
Dropped in Rotation C of Orphix Venus Proxima or Veil Proxima missions.
Awarded at Tier 9 in The Duviri Paradox Circuit for Week 1.
**Notes:**
Additively stacks with mods that increase fire rate (e.g. Speed Trigger).
Does not double its bonus for bows

Can be triggered by critical hits from almost any source, including all weapon types, beast and sentinel attacks, and even Warframe abilities (such as Wisp's Breach Surge sparks and all of Gyre's damaging abilities)

## Arcane Battery

### Wiki
Arcane Battery is an Arcane Enhancement for Warframes that increases maximum energy per point of armor, up to 1,000 additional energy.
Effect
Arcane Battery can be obtained from Ascension's optional Sister of Parvos, summoned by acquiring 3 Sister Beacons during the elevator ride. Alternatively it can be purchased from Ordis in the Drifter's Camp for 10 Vestigial Motes, for a total of 210 for max rank.
Dropped by Captain Vor in Tolstoj (Mercury) in The Steel Path.
**Notes:**
Maximum energy changes dynamically based on any changes to armor.
As such, being inflicted with a Corrosive or Heat status effect will remove armor and some of your bonus energy, requiring you to regain it.
Heat procs are far more common and more deadly, as they will remove up to half of your total armor while burning.
Health Conversion is unwieldy on Simulacrum and might look like it isn't properly updating, but it does work mostly reliably in normal missions.
If armor is reduced, such as losing a stack of Health Conversion, any energy overflow will immediately disappear.
When combined with Preparation or similar effects, the increased energy maximum is applied first, resulting in a higher starting energy as well.
**Tips:**
Works well with mods like Quick Thinking and Rage.
Qorvex, Valkyr Prime, and Kullervo have high base armor that grants them effective use of this arcane.

## Melee Afflictions

### Wiki
Melee Afflictions is a Melee Arcane that adds additional stacks to existing Status Effects on enemies that are knocked down or flung by melee attacks.
Effect
Melee Afflictions can be obtained from Ascension's optional Sister of Parvos, summoned by acquiring 3 Sister Beacons during the elevator ride. Alternatively it can be purchased from Ordis in the Drifter's Camp for 10 Vestigial Motes, for a total of 210 for max rank.
Damage Calculation
For damaging status effects, each time Melee Afflictions is triggered, the damage of each added stack is calculated as follows. There are 4 main parts, 1. Damage of individual Status procs, 2. The average of all Status procs for each Status Effect, 3. The sum of all the average Status Effect damage, 4. The calculation of new Status Effect damages for procs that are present on the target.
For each Status proc find the Modded Base Damage of every Status proc on the enemy for each Status Effect type.
Additional Multipliers include modded critical multiplier on Critical Hit and multipliers on Enemy Body Parts; these stack multiplicatively with each other.
Note that Faction Damage Bonuses only apply once from the source weapon of the status. The "double dip" is not counted.
For each Status Effect type, sum the damage of all Status Effect procs is used to find the damage of that Status Effect.
Affliction Damage Hit is the instance of damage caused by Melee Afflictions and is the sum of all of the Status Effect Average Damage and multiplied by melee Faction Damage Bonuses.
Affliction Damage Hit is used to find the Damage Over Time of all Status Effect types present. Modifiers that are consider are the same as the normal calculation for respective Status Effect types. Faction Damage Bonuses, and Status Damage Bonuses that are present on the melee weapon are applied to Status Effects.

## Virtuos Spike

### Wiki
Virtuos Spike is an Arcane Enhancement for Operator Amps that converts Void damage to Puncture.
Effect
**Acquisition:**
Can be bought from Vox Solaris for 5,000 Standing upon reaching the rank of Agent.
**Notes:**
Amps can still destroy Kuva clouds.
Rank 3 conversion percent is actually 96%, not 98% as shown in-game.
When using multiple conversion arcanes, the damage is determined by base damage of the amp multiplied by the conversion percent of the arcane divided by the sum of all the conversion percent. This value is then multiplied by the lesser of the following two values: the sum of the conversion % or 98%.
For example, using a max rank Virtuos Spike (96% conversion) with a rank 2 Virtuos Forge (72% conversion) on a Rahn Prism will result in
1000 * 96% / (96% + 72%) * 98% Puncture damage and
1000 * 72% / (96% + 72%) * 98% Heat damage.
