## Boot
Synthetic fibers and a molded foam insole were the basis for what was once a robust and comfortable combat boot. Now, it's a home for tiny aquatic organisms.

### Wiki
In-Game Description
The Boot is a rare possible catch while fishing in the Plains of Eidolon. Whenever the player lands a near hit (grazing the outermost edges of a fish's hitbox with their fishing spear) or hits the floor of their fishing location, there is a chance they will catch a boot, easily identified beforehand by the sound of the spear hitting something, but without the hit marker or any damage numbers popping up. The fish that triggered the boot will not despawn after you catch the boot.
The Boot can be sold for 400 from one's inventory or can be used to craft a Boot Trophy.
**Notes:**
Due to its standardized fabrication the boot is the only "fish" without size and randomized weight, always being exactly 4.0kg.
Instead of the usual uplifting music track accompanying each catch, a boot will be presented with a sarcastic banjo jingle playing in the back:

Even though the player only catches one boot, as a reward they would still get a pair and will both remain in the inventory. This was later fixed.
This easter egg comes from the fairly-known and used reference The Legend of Zelda : Twilight Princess, where the player may catch a boot when fishing.
